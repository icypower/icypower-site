## Goal of the website
Leads (WhatsApp messages, calls, contact-form submissions) for:
1. **Companies:** team-building days, company fun days, HR wellness events
   (`/business`, `/wellness-day`).
2. **Private groups:** friends, families, bachelor/bachelorette parties,
   birthdays (`/private-groups`, `/events`, `/evening-retreat`).
Secondary: open workshops (`/open-session`, `/booking`) and couples.

## Audience
HR managers, office managers and team leads planning a team day; people
organising a group outing, bachelorette or birthday. Hebrew speakers in Israel.

## Facts from the site FAQ
- No experience needed; guided step by step, any fitness level.
- Water 3-8°C; 1-5 minutes in the tub depending on the person.
- Not suitable: pregnancy, heart conditions, epilepsy. Everyone signs a short
  health declaration.
- Bring a swimsuit, towel, flip-flops and warm clothes. Icy Power brings the
  tubs, ice and equipment.
- Any group size, from a couple to hundreds.

## Brand voice
Relaxation, fun, connection, practical stress-management tools, a touch of
luxury. Not extreme/adrenaline, not spiritual/clinical. Natural Hebrew, no em
dashes, no AI buzzwords, no medical promises.
